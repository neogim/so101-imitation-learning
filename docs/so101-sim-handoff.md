# SO-101 MuJoCo / SAC continuation guide

Last reviewed: 2026-11-12 (Asia/Tokyo). This document is a **time-stamped handoff**, not an assertion of current live repo status. Consult `AGENTS.md` first.

## Scope and current scientific distinction

SO-101 is being studied with a saved Real-to-Sim Episode 4 layout and DLS / SAC reach trajectories. The current **J5 ablation video** is generated by assigning recorded/learned joint values to `data.qpos` and calling `mujoco.mj_forward()`, without dynamic integration or contact response. It therefore cannot block floor penetration or push the Muhi object or cup. A 10 mm TCP positional success is not a physical grasp or collision-avoidance result. The recorded visual home pose exceeds imported URDF limits and must not be sent to hardware.

The prior investigation found spurious base–shoulder contact resulting from convex-hull approximation of the source meshes. A diagnostic split-convex prototype improved initial overlap but did **not** establish stable PD tracking or validated collision safety. Preserve and reuse this evidence rather than restarting from zero.

2. Build a **separate, opt-in physics demonstration**, keeping the kinematic J5 comparison and existing SAC checkpoint unchanged. Add accurate-enough table/floor collision geoms, a Muhi dynamic free body with reasonable provisional mass/inertia/friction, and a cup with separate bottom and walls (fixed unless motion is needed).
3. Implement physical stepping (`mujoco.mj_step`) and bounded actuator/PD target tracking instead of directly teleporting the five robot joint qpos each frame. Deal explicitly with the visual home pose outside joint limits, using a valid initial physics pose.
4. Verify in stages: falling free object rests on tabletop; robot/table contact is detected and yields a physical response; pushing Muhi moves it; Muhi collides with cup wall/bottom. Track contact pairs, max penetration, free-body motion, and joint tracking. Keep time step, gains and contact parameters documented.
5. Do not claim full grasp/place or SAC transfer into the physical environment until actually validated; training the current kinematic SAC policy is a separate choice.
